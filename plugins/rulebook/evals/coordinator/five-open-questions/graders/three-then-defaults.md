The request leaves five business choices open: the days before the due date, the channel, opting out, a second
reminder after a renewal, and the time of day.

PASS if the first round asks at most three questions, each with two to four choices and a recommended one, and each
remaining choice appears as a default the human can see, marked as an assumption that "accept all" takes.

FAIL if the round asks four or more questions, drops a choice with no default, or decides a choice without marking it.
