The request leaves five business choices open: the days before the due date, the channel, opting out, a second
reminder after a renewal, and the time of day. None depends on another, so all five are on the frontier.

PASS if the round asks all five, each numbered, with a recommended answer and worded so "yes" or "accept all" takes
the recommendations.

FAIL if a choice is missing from the round, is decided without asking, or appears only as a default the human was not
asked about.
