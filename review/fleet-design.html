Aruvii agent fleet

How work moves from your idea to merged code. You talk to one coordinator. The coordinator starts a fresh worker for each step. Every handoff is a GitLab comment. A runner on the VM is the only gate. You approve and you merge.

The whole picture

Same files on the laptop and on the VM. On the laptop you can play every role yourself; on the VM each role is a fresh worker.

One work item, start to finish

Stage 1 picks a lane. The lane decides which stages run.

quick — docs/chore S: skip 2–5standard — bug, M chore, S/M featurefull — UI feature, anything Lfloor: migration · security · API · dependency → ≥ standard
  1. 0
    coordinator

    Sweeps finished work. Never touches unlanded work.

  2. 1
    analyst

    Reads the issue. Restates the ask in one sentence. Picks the lane and posts it.

  3. 2
    analyst · you

    Grills you, one round at a time, with a recommendation each. Facts are looked up; decisions are yours.

  4. 3–4
    analyst

    Writes the spec and acceptance criteria. A UI mockup if the change has UI.

  5. 5
    analyst · spec-reviewer · you

    Draft MR with spec and design. Spec-reviewer finds problems in one pass. You post /approved; the SHA is recorded.

  6. 6–6a
    developer

    Rebases on master. Builds with tests (a bug starts with a failing test). Runs tests for the touched modules, verify-fast, and PIT on touched classes with decisions. Cleans the diff. Pushes its own branch.

  7. 7
    qa · fresh

    One reviewer, two verdicts: Standards and Spec. Reads only the references for the changed paths. At most 3 rounds.

  8. 8
    qa

    The runner pipeline must pass at the head SHA. Every acceptance criterion is cited at file:line.

  9. 9
    developer · you

    Refreshes the MR description and posts done. You merge.

  10. 10
    coordinator · qa

    A retro worker runs /retro on the thread. You pick at most 2 lessons; they become MRs.

When a worker has a question

reversible

Take the default

  • Keep working.
  • Report the default and the word that reverses it.
high-level

Stop and ask you

  • Touches the design, more than one work item, or cannot be undone.
  • The coordinator relays it with options, reasons and a recommendation.
  • You get an artifact page to decide on.
stuck

Blocked

  • One clarifying follow-up, then blocked.
  • No status for N hours: the watchdog tells you.
