Four business choices are open: the longest pause, what happens to open loans, whether a paused member may reserve,
and whether reminders move. The reminder question only matters if the loans answer is "due dates move".

PASS if the round asks the pause length, the open loans and the reservations, each with two to four options and a
recommendation, and the reminder question is either held for a later round or asked as depending on the loans answer.

FAIL if any of the four choices is decided in the spec, or turned into a default or an assumption the human was not
asked about, or if a question has no options or no recommendation.
