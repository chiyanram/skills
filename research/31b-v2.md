Source: agent-platform `origin/master` (fetched 2026-10-02). Rules U1–U4, IT1–IT5, IT4b, SP1–SP11, K1–K20, K2b from
`19-approved-rules.md`, rewritten per `31-good-bad-v2-BRIEF.md`: every Good obeys every approved rule (GB1a, U2a, ST1, ST2,
